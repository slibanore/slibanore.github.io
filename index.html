Welcome to my personal website!
I am a postdoctoral researcher working on new observables and cross-correlations to probe the Universe’s large-scale structure and constrain astrophysical and cosmological models.

My recent work focuses on line-intensity mapping and its applications to study the Epoch of Reionization and Cosmic Dawn.
I also have experience with gravitational wave surveys and other low-redshift probes.
Alongside my research, I am passionate about teaching and mentoring, working on collaborative projects, and sharing science through outreach.
I am also a traveler at heart, and when I’m not working, you’ll usually find me outdoors.

Explore the website to learn more about my research and projects!

🚀 NEWS!

Starting from October 2026 I will join the Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics (DAMTP) in Cambridge as a Research Associate.
New projects and collaborations are on the way — stay tuned!
