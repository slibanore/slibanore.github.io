I'm a postdoc researcher in astrophysics and cosmology.
My work focuses on new observables and cross-correlations.

Currently, I'm interested in line-intensity mapping and its applications to explore the epoch of reionization and cosmic dawn.
I also have experience with gravitational waves and large-scale structure tracers.

I'm passionate about teaching and mentoring, collaborative research, and outreach.
