Outreach, to me, isn’t optional: it’s part of the job.
Science is too exciting to stay hidden in the academic world or buried in journal papers. Whether we’re talking about astrophysics, climate change, or medical research, scientists carry a social responsibility: our work doesn’t just shape knowledge, it shapes society.
Raising awareness, sharing the wonder of discovery, sparking curiosity, and helping people develop critical thinking are crucial activities in today's world.

I also simply love doing outreach, especially with children and teenagers, who ask the best (and often the hardest!) questions. It’s so important to spark their curiosity and their sense of wonder about the world and its complexity.

As Carl Sagan said: “The visions we offer our children shape the future. It matters what those visions are. Often they become self-fulfilling prophecies. Dreams are maps.”

For this reason, I have always been involved in outreach projects and collaborations.

PLaNCK! Magazine


PLaNCK! is a science magazine for children (ages 8–12), published in Italian with some content also available in English. Each issue is dedicated to a single theme explored from many different angles and perspectives. Some of my favourite issues cover space missions, the scientific method, science & fantasy, and travel & science.

www.planck-magazine.it

Every issue is produced in collaboration with field experts, and topics are brought to life through articles, interviews, comics, and hands-on experiments.
What makes PLaNCK! special is its collaboration with schools: each issue is reviewed directly by children, ensuring the magazine truly speaks their language and curiosity.

I am part of the editorial board: I write articles, contribute to the revision process, coordinate school collaborations, and help manage the magazine’s social media communication.
